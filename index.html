About EQRIA

Creative Thinking.
Meaningful Impact.

Studio Leadership Founders

Founded by Ashirul & Tanvir

EQRIA was established with a singular creative conviction: modern digital brands win when sharp strategic thinking meets unmistakable visual craft. Together, Ashirul and Tanvir lead every engagement with hands-on creative direction.

EQRIA is a modern creative digital agency built for ambitious brands navigating fast-moving social and video platforms. We bridge the gap between aesthetic precision and performance-driven communication.

In an era of endless scrolling, generic templates fade into the background. Our studio crafts intentional visual systems—from thumb-stopping social ad creatives and high-CTR YouTube thumbnails to creator-paced AI UGC ads and fluid motion graphics—designed to capture attention in the first second and elevate brand perception over time.
